What it does

Sales funnels and selling. The funnel builder is the historical core: opt-in and sales funnels with 1-click upsells, order bumps, deadline funnels, and A/B tests, connected to Stripe and PayPal with 0% platform transaction fees (your payment processor's standard fees still apply). It also sells physical products, with shipping and inventory management, which most creator platforms bolt on or skip.

Websites and blogs. Websites, blogs, and a mobile link-in-bio page are built in, with custom domains from the free plan (1 domain on Free). This is what makes systeme.io a plausible home for the whole business rather than a checkout layer in front of a site hosted elsewhere.

Email marketing. Campaigns, newsletters, tags, automation rules, and workflows are included on every plan, with unlimited sends. The honest caveat is depth: automation rules are rationed by tier (1 on Free, 10 on Startup, unlimited only on Unlimited), and the system is built for funnel follow-ups and straightforward sequences rather than elaborate behavior-based branching. That trade-off is the whole story of our systeme.io vs Kit comparison — if email automation sophistication is where your revenue comes from, a specialist pulls ahead.

Courses and communities. Course hosting and communities are part of the same subscription, capped by tier (1 course on Free, 5 on Startup, 20 on Webinar, unlimited on Unlimited). Having the course, the community, and the emails that sell them under one login — and one bill — is the all-in-one proposition at its strongest, because these are exactly the pieces a course seller would otherwise rent from three vendors.

Your own affiliate program. Every plan, including Free, can run an affiliate program for your own products and plug it into your funnels and websites. This is a genuinely unusual inclusion: affiliate management is normally a separate paid tool, and for creators whose growth plan is "other people promote my course," it removes a line item entirely.

The rest of the table. Evergreen webinars arrive on the Webinar plan. CRM pipelines, events and bookings, coupon codes, and a long integrations list (Stripe, PayPal, Google tools, Zoom, and more) round out the pricing page. Free migration is offered on all plans, and support is 24/7 by email.

Who should pick it — and who should skip it

Choose systeme.io if: you are launching a side business, a course, or a faceless creator brand and need funnels, a course, a community, and email before you have revenue; you want one flat, predictable bill instead of per-subscriber pricing that grows with your list; you plan to sell courses or run a community; or you want to recruit affiliates for your own products without buying affiliate software. The path is unusually low-risk: start free, and the first paid decision is $17.

Also skip it if you are primarily a physical-goods store (a dedicated ecommerce platform will serve you better), if your business already runs well on separate best-in-class tools, or if you are wary of exit costs: funnels, courses, and a community built inside an all-in-one are the pieces that are slowest to move out later, which is worth weighing before you build everything in one place.

FAQ

What does the free plan actually include?
Up to 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels, 1 blog, 1 course, 1 custom domain, and 1 affiliate program, with unlimited email sends. The tight caps are 1 automation rule and 500 students. It never expires and needs no credit card, so it works as a genuine launch tier — but a growing course or list will push you to Startup at $17/month.

Does systeme.io take a cut of my sales?
No platform transaction fee: 0% on all plans, including Free. You still pay your payment processor (Stripe or PayPal) its standard processing fees — systeme.io adds nothing on top.

Which plan should I choose?
By contact count, per the official guidance: under 2,000 contacts, Free; 2,000–5,000, Startup ($17); 5,000–10,000, or if you want evergreen webinars, Webinar ($47); over 10,000 contacts or when you need unlimited funnels and automation rules, Unlimited ($97). Also check the non-contact caps — automation rules are what push many small sellers from Free to Startup before contacts do.

What happens if I go over my plan's limits?
You get a warning email each day for three days, plus a dashboard alert, and time to upgrade or remove the excess. On a paid plan, day four brings an automatic upgrade to the next tier so nothing breaks. On the free plan, the account is restricted instead: opt-in forms and payments stop and automations pause until you upgrade or get back within limits. Nothing is deleted either way.

Can I cancel or move away later?
You can upgrade or cancel at any time from account settings; a cancelled paid plan runs to the end of the billing period and then reverts to the free plan rather than vanishing. systeme.io also advertises free migration in, which lowers the cost of arriving. Leaving is the standard all-in-one caveat: email lists export routinely, but funnels, courses, and communities are rebuilds, not exports.

Bottom line

systeme.io's pitch is arithmetic, not magic: the tools a small online business rents separately — funnels, email, courses, community, affiliate management — come in one subscription whose most expensive tier is $97/month and whose entry tier is free. The published pricing backs the pitch, with two honest qualifications: the automation caps mean power email marketers will outgrow the middle tiers' depth before they outgrow their limits, and everything built inside an all-in-one is slower to leave than a tool stack. For a creator or side project starting from zero infrastructure, it is the easiest platform to justify.
