Pricing: four tiers, priced flat

beehiiv renamed its plan lineup: the tiers are now Free, Lite, Pro, and Enterprise (older reviews quoting Launch, Scale, and Max are working from the previous structure). The pricing shape is the story. Where Kit and MailerLite bill on a per-subscriber ladder, beehiiv charges a flat tier price up to a ceiling so high that most newsletters never touch it: Lite is $49/month billed yearly whether you have 3,000 subscribers or 90,000.

Two mechanics decide value here. First, the free plan is a real home for a starting newsletter: 2,500 subscribers, unlimited sends, a website, and the recommendation network, free forever with no credit card. Second, the jump from free to Lite is not about headroom — it is about monetization and automation: paid subscriptions, ad monetization, automations, and A/B testing all start at Lite. Pro's $95 buys scale comforts (10 seats, 10 publications, advanced analytics, webhooks, branding removal, 24/7 priority support) rather than necessities for a solo publisher. Yearly billing saves up to 20% against paying month to month.

What it does

Publishing and website. Every plan — free included — bundles the full product set: the newsletter itself, a website for it, podcast and community features, and digital products. beehiiv's own framing is that moving up changes limits, support, and advanced features, not which products you can use. For a creator whose "website" is the newsletter archive and a subscribe page, this retires a separate site builder from day one.

Growth. The recommendation network is beehiiv's signature: publications recommend each other to their readers, and the boost marketplace extends the same idea. This is the feature with no real equivalent in a classic email tool — growth is treated as part of the platform, not something you import from elsewhere.

Monetization. Paid subscriptions and digital product sales carry a 0% platform take rate — you keep the revenue minus Stripe's standard processing fee (2.9% + $0.30). Ad monetization, where sponsors are matched to your newsletter, starts on Lite. Together with the recommendation network, this is the "newsletter as a media business" toolkit: audience growth and two revenue lines in one subscription.

Automation and analytics. Automations, audience polls, and A/B testing start on Lite; analytics lookback grows from 30 days on Free to 12 months on Lite and unlimited on Pro, which also adds webhooks and dynamic content. The honest qualification: beehiiv's automation is publishing-grade, not commerce-grade — creators who run launches and buyer segmentation on tags should compare against Kit before committing (see the comparison).

Who should pick it — and who should skip it

Choose beehiiv if: you are starting a newsletter from zero and want the free tier to carry you furthest (2,500 subscribers, unlimited sends, a website included); the newsletter is the business and you want recommendations, paid subscriptions, and ads in one place; or your list is growing fast and a flat $49 up to 100,000 subscribers beats watching a per-subscriber bill climb every month.

FAQ

What does the free plan actually include?
Up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited email sends, 1 publication and 1 seat, a website, landing pages, email templates, and access to the recommendation network. It is free forever and needs no credit card. What it lacks is monetization and automation: paid subscriptions, ad monetization, and automations start on Lite.

What is the difference between Lite and Pro?
Headroom and polish. Lite ($49/month billed yearly) covers up to 100,000 subscribers, 2 seats, and 3 publications, and unlocks paid subscriptions, ad monetization, automations, and A/B testing. Pro ($95/month billed yearly) raises the ceiling to 250,000 subscribers, 10 seats, and 10 publications, and adds branding removal, advanced analytics with unlimited lookback, webhooks, and 24/7 priority support.

Does beehiiv take a cut of my revenue?
No platform cut: beehiiv takes 0% of paid subscription and digital product revenue. You pay Stripe's standard processing fee (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction) on top, as on most platforms.

Which plan should I choose?
Start free — it holds a real newsletter. Move to Lite when you want to charge for subscriptions, run ads, or automate; for most solo publishers Lite is the resting point, since its 100,000-subscriber ceiling is far away. Choose Pro when you run multiple publications, need a team of seats, or want your own branding with no beehiiv marks.

Can I move away later?
Subscriber lists are exportable as a standard email list, so the audience moves with you. The rebuild cost is the usual one for a platform with a built-in website: posts and pages published inside beehiiv would need to be recreated on the next platform.

Bottom line

beehiiv's pitch is a media business in a box: publish, grow through recommendations, and monetize through subscriptions and ads, priced flat so the bill does not punish growth. The free plan is the most generous starting tier among the newsletter platforms we cover, and Lite at $49/month billed yearly is the natural home for a newsletter that has started earning. The honest qualifications are automation depth — publishing-grade rather than commerce-grade — and a per-subscriber comparison that only favors beehiiv once a list is large or growing fast.
