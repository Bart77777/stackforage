1. ElevenLabs — up to 55% off annual plans (ends December 1)

What it's for: AI voice generation — text to speech, voice cloning, and dubbing for faceless channels, audiobooks, podcasts, and localized content. Our full breakdown is in the ElevenLabs review.

The deal: from November 16 to December 1, annual plans are discounted by up to 55%. The two prices that matter for creators: Creator is $99 for the first year (standard annual price $220), and Pro is $445.50 for the first year. Creator is the tier most individual creators land on — it includes Professional Voice Cloning, a commercial license, and a monthly credit pool good for roughly two hours of narration.

Who it suits: anyone already planning a year of paid ElevenLabs. At $99, Creator costs less than five months at the monthly rate, so the break-even against paying monthly arrives quickly.

The honest caveat: this is a first-year price on annual billing. Year two renews at the standard annual rate, and the credit pool still expires monthly — a discounted year does not bank unused credits. ElevenLabs is also audio-only: it will not edit or assemble your videos.

2. Fliki — 50% off annual Standard & Premium (code FLIKIBFCM50)

What it's for: turning text — a script, a blog post, a one-line idea — into a finished video with AI voiceover, visuals, music, and captions, aimed at faceless YouTube, TikTok, and Shorts production. Details in our Fliki review and the Fliki vs Pictory comparison.

The deal: Fliki's official pricing page is running a Black Friday banner: 50% off annual Standard and Premium plans with code FLIKIBFCM50, applied at checkout. Standard is the creator tier (180 credits a month, 1080p, voice cloning, commercial rights); Premium raises the pool to 600 credits and adds AI video clips and API access.

Who it suits: creators who publish on a schedule and know they will use the credits every month. At half price, a year of Standard costs what six monthly payments would — but only if the output actually ships.

The honest caveat: Fliki does not publish the sale's end date on its pricing page, so treat it as ending without notice and do not plan around a specific last day. The deeper caveat is the meter: credits are charged per generation, so heavy re-editing eats the allowance the discount is applied to. A cheap year of credits you burn on re-takes is not cheap.

3. MailerLite — 26% off annual plans (code LITEFRIDAY26, ends December 18)

What it's for: email marketing for small lists — campaigns, automations, signup forms, and landing pages, with digital-product selling built in. See our MailerLite review.

The deal: MailerLite's "Lite Friday" sale runs November 17 to December 18: 26% off annual Comfort and Power plans with code LITEFRIDAY26 at checkout. On the official sale page, Comfort starts at $107/year (down from $144/year) and Power at $222/year (down from $300/year), with the same 26% at every subscriber tier. The discount is held for one year from purchase, and if your list outgrows the plan mid-year, the discount carries over to the larger plan pro rata.

Who it suits: anyone about to pay for a year of email tooling anyway, especially lists expecting to grow — the pro-rata transfer means growth does not void the saving. Of the four deals here, this is also the only one that runs well past Black Friday weekend, so there is no need to rush it.

The honest caveat: 26% is a modest cut, and it is not automatic — forget the code at checkout and you pay full price. It applies to annual plans only, existing annual subscribers get it only when upgrading to a higher tier, and accounts billed through Shopify are excluded.

What it's for: newsletters as a business — publishing, a website, paid subscriptions at a 0% platform fee, an ad network, and a recommendation network for growth. See our beehiiv review.

The deal: beehiiv's official Black Friday page offers 20% off any annual plan with code BFCM2026 at checkout. The discounted price lasts one billing cycle — one year. Monthly subscribers can switch to annual to use it, and annual Lite subscribers get 20% off an upgrade to annual Pro.

Who it suits: newsletter writers who have already decided beehiiv is their platform and were going to commit to a year. On Pro at larger list sizes, 20% is a meaningful amount of money even though the percentage looks small.

How not to waste money on any of these

Do the renewal math, not the discount math. Every deal here is a first-year price. The right comparison is "discounted year one + standard year two" against what you would otherwise pay — not the percentage in the headline.

Only prepay for tools already in your workflow. An annual plan is a commitment to a year of use. A 50% discount on a tool you abandon in February is a 100% loss on the remaining months.

Enter the code before you pay. Fliki, MailerLite, and beehiiv all require the code at checkout, and none of them applies it retroactively as a matter of course. Check that the discounted total is on screen before completing payment.

FAQ

When do these Black Friday deals end?
ElevenLabs ends December 1 and MailerLite ends December 18 — both dates are published by the vendors. Fliki and beehiiv do not publish an end date on their official pages, so treat both as short windows and confirm at checkout.

Do the discounts apply to monthly plans?
No. All four deals are annual-billing only. Monthly subscribers can switch to an annual plan to take part, which is precisely the commitment the discount is buying.

Do the discounted prices renew?
No. Each discount covers the first year (one annual billing cycle). Renewal is at the standard annual rate unless another promotion is running at that time.

Why are there only four deals here?
Because only four of the eight programs we track have an offer we could verify on the vendor's own website on November 21, 2026. We do not list coupon-site codes we cannot confirm.

Bottom line

If you were already going to pay for one of these tools, this is the cheapest week of the year to do it: ElevenLabs Creator at $99 and Fliki at half price are the two standouts for video creators, MailerLite's 26% is the patient option with a December 18 deadline and a discount that survives plan upgrades, and beehiiv's 20% is worth taking only after checking it beats any discount already on your account. If you were not going to pay for any of them, the correct Black Friday move is to buy none of them — every discount here expires, but the annual commitment does not.
