Pricing: the meter matters more than the sticker

Fliki's two paid plans look simple — Standard at $28/user/month ($21 billed yearly) and Premium at $88/user/month ($66 billed yearly) — but the real price is set by the credit meter. Credits are charged on the duration of audio generated, at roughly half a credit per minute of finished content on Fliki's own estimator, and the charge applies again whenever you change the script, the voice, or the pauses and re-generate. Fliki's FAQ puts Standard's 180 monthly credits at roughly 12 medium five-minute videos if you do not re-generate much; a channel that iterates on scripts will land well below that.

What it does

Text to video, several ways in. Ideas, scripts, blog posts, and presentations all convert into videos, with stock images, video clips, and music included, plus AI image generation. Paid plans unlock the premium media library; Premium adds generated AI video clips and photo avatars.

Voices are the product. The voice library is the deepest in this category on published specs: 1,000 voices already on Standard, more than 2,000 on Premium, across 80+ languages and 100+ dialects, with translation into those languages on paid plans. Voice cloning starts on Standard (1 clone; 3 on Premium), so a channel can lock in one consistent narrator voice on the cheapest paid tier — the feature Pictory holds back for its mid-tier plan.

Series and characters. Two features aim squarely at channels rather than one-off videos: custom characters for a recurring on-screen identity, and Series automation (1 series on Standard, 3 on Premium) paired with direct YouTube publishing (50 publications a month on Standard, 120 on Premium).

Rights. Every paid plan carries commercial rights, and Fliki states you can publish on YouTube and monetize, provided your text content is original. The free plan carries neither commercial rights nor a clean export — the watermark stays on.

Who should pick it — and who should skip it

Choose Fliki if: narration quality and variety drive your channel; you publish in a non-English language or several languages; you want a cloned narrator voice without jumping to a mid-tier plan; you run a recurring series and want the automation and direct publishing; or you want to validate a format on a permanent free plan before spending anything.

Try Fliki

Skip Fliki if: your editing style means re-generating narration many times per video — the credit meter taxes exactly that habit, and a minutes-based meter like Pictory's is easier to predict (see the head-to-head comparison). Also skip it if your videos start as finished articles and premium stock footage (Getty Images, Storyblocks) is the bottleneck, or if the voice inside your current tool is already fine and what you actually need is a standalone voice upgrade — that is ElevenLabs, from $6/month.

FAQ

How many videos can I make on Standard?
Fliki's own estimate: Standard's 180 credits cover roughly 60 one-minute videos, 12 five-minute videos, or 6 ten-minute videos — assuming you do not re-generate much. Every script or voice change that triggers new audio costs credits again, so heavy editors should budget below the estimate.

What is the longest video I can make?
Per Fliki's pricing FAQ, 15 minutes on Standard and 30 minutes on Premium. Some of Fliki's plan summaries still show 40 minutes for Premium, so if length is decisive for you, verify the limit in the app before committing to annual billing.

Can I monetize YouTube videos made with Fliki?
Yes, on paid plans: they include commercial rights, and Fliki explicitly allows publishing on YouTube and monetizing, provided your text content is original. The free plan has no commercial rights. YouTube's own monetization policies still apply on top.

Which plan should I choose?
Start on the free plan to prove the format. Choose Standard if one cloned voice, 1080p, and up to 15-minute videos cover your channel — it is the realistic resting point for most faceless channels at $21/month billed yearly. Choose Premium if you need the deeper voice library, AI video clips, API access, or videos up to 30 minutes.

Can I cancel or change plans later?
Fliki sells its paid plans per user with no commitment on monthly billing — upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from the account's billing settings. Annual billing is the discount route (25% off), so it suits a format you have already validated.

Bottom line

Fliki is the voice-first end of the AI video market: the deepest voice and language library on published specs, cloning on the entry paid tier, series automation for channels, and a free plan that is actually useful for testing. Its honest qualifications are the credit meter — priced on generation, not on finished video, so it punishes iteration — and a length limit you should verify in the app if long videos are your format. If your channel is narration-led or non-English, it is the easiest generator to justify; if your channel is article-led, read the Fliki vs Pictory comparison before deciding.
