About StackForage

StackForage helps creators and small online businesses choose the software they run on — AI tools, newsletter platforms, and all-in-one selling tools.

How we compare

Every comparison on this site is built from the tools' official documentation, published pricing, and plan terms, checked at the time of writing and re-checked when terms change. We organize the differences that actually matter when you are choosing: what you pay as you grow, what is locked behind higher tiers, and who should skip the tool entirely.

We do not publish invented hands-on claims, fake screenshots of results, or earnings promises. Where a judgment is an opinion, we label it as one and show the facts it rests on.

How this site makes money

Some links on StackForage are affiliate links. If you sign up through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes the price you pay, and it does not buy a better verdict — tools we would not recommend do not get recommended. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.
