Media Kit

StackForage is an English-language comparison and review site covering AI tools and creator software. We help creators and small businesses choose the tools they run on: newsletter platforms, AI writing and video tools, and all-in-one selling tools.

What we publish

Reviews and side-by-side comparisons built from official documentation, published pricing, and plan terms, verified at the time of writing. Alongside the site, we publish a Substack newsletter and daily posts on X and Pinterest.

Audience

Creators, newsletter writers, and small online businesses (1–100 people) choosing or switching software, mainly in North America, Europe, and Australia. Readers arrive with a purchase decision in progress: they are comparing plans, prices, and limits.

How we work with partners

We work on affiliate commission only. We do not charge for content production, and paid placement does not buy a better verdict. We do not use brand bidding, coupon sites, or incentivized traffic. Every article that contains affiliate links is covered by our Affiliate Disclosure.
